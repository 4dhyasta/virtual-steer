# virtual-steer

Turn your iPhone into a steering wheel for your PC.

## Tutorial

---

1. Download the `.zip` file from the [**Release Page**](https://github.com/4dhyasta/virtual-steer/releases/tag/virtual-steer).
2. Extract the `.zip` file.
3. Install the `.ipa` file from the extracted folder onto your iPhone using [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/), [SideStore](https://sidestore.io/), or [AltStore](https://altstore.io/).
4. Connect your iPhone to your PC using a USB cable.
5. Launch `start.bat`
6. Enjoy!

## Additional

---

- **Control layout:** From left to right — Brake Slider, Gear Down, Gear Up, Accelerate Slider.
- **Centering the axis:** Hold your iPhone horizontally in your hands, then press both gear buttons simultaneously.
- **Opening the menu:** Press and hold both gear buttons simultaneously to open the menu. The menu is in Indonesian, so use Google Translate if needed.
